# trade-agreements-and-economic-growth
# Do Trade Agreements Translate Into Growth?

This research explores the relationship between trade agreements, trade openness, and economic growth across countries, with a focus on India's trade relationships. Using secondary data from sources such as the World Bank, WTO, UNCTAD, and IMF, the study examines trade volumes, export patterns, and economic performance to understand whether increased trade translates into stronger growth.

The analysis highlights how trade outcomes vary across countries and why greater trade openness does not always lead to higher economic growth.
